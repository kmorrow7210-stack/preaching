1. **Series Placement & Call to Stand**
   - Place this passage in the series (last week, where we are, what is next).
   - "Go with me to... If you'll stand with me for the reading of the Word."
2. **Opening Scripture Reading (KJV)**
   - `[Paste verified verbatim KJV text here]`
3. **Title & Pulpit Prayer**
   - Announce title right before prayer.
   - Short pulpit prayer asking for anointing ("Would you pray for me...").
4. **Congregation Sits**
   - "You may be seated in the presence of the Lord."

---

## 2. Hook / Intro Story (~5 min / ~550 words)

*(Comes AFTER reading, prayer, and sitting. Never before Scripture.)*

- **True story with real names, dates, and numbers** (leave the twist unresolved until the closing bookend).
- **Upsetting the Equilibrium ("Oops"):** Surface the tension and bridge directly into the biblical passage.

---

## 3. Movement 1: [Scene / Setting] (~7 min / ~770 words)

- Retell the scene verse by verse (cite verses aloud, NIV wording).
- Brief ancient / Second Temple context in a sentence or two.
- **Discrepancy ("Ugh"):** Name the deeper human or cosmic tension.
- Mid-story pastoral bridge ("Have you ever been there?").

## 4. Movement 2: [Complication / Pattern] (~8 min / ~880 words)

- Whole-Bible pattern or "same word, same author" link.
- 1 to 2 original-language words explained in plain English.
- Supporting NIV Scripture quotations (verified).
- Refrain check-in (#1 / #2).

## 5. Movement 3: [The Turn / Gospel Reveal] (~8 min / ~880 words)

- **The Turn ("Aha"):** How Jesus / the Spirit reframes the entire conflict.
- Controlling contrast sharpened into 1 to 2 quotable aphorisms built on a turn.
- Concrete list of ordinary burdens + reversal litany.
- Honesty about lingering pain or unanswered prayer where the text touches it.

---

## 6. Closing Bookend & Protected Landing (~4 min / ~440 words)

- `[PROTECTED]` **Return to the opening story with the twist that lands the big idea.**
- `[PROTECTED]` **Final statement of the contrast and refrain.**
- Closing cue to congregation and musician ("If you'll stand with me, I'm closing.").

## 7. Open Altar & Closing Prayer (~5 min / ~550 words)

- `[PROTECTED]` **"This altar is open. I want to pray for you."**
- **Altar response shaped by the sermon's own image** (2 to 3 invited groups):
  1. Those who need to be saved or come back.
  2. Believers who are worn out, grieving, or carrying a burden named in the message.
  3. Specific group tied to this sermon's image.
- **Closing prayer** weaving the sermon's images back into prayer.
