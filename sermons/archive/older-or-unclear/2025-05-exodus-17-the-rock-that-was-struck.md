The Rock That Was Struck
Core Texts: Exodus 17:1-7; John 7:37-39; John 19:34; Numbers 20:1-13
Overall Theme: God meets us in our thirst and doubt not with contactless solutions, but with
His personal presence, ultimately revealed in Christ, the Rock struck once for all, from whom
living water (the Holy Spirit) now flows freely by grace.

Introduction: If You Were There—Banks of the Nile
● Image: Nile – life & decay, dependence (Herodotus: "gift of the Nile").
● Contrast: Egypt's brilliance (stars, pyramids, papyrus) vs. ritualistic dependence
(throwing gold, praying for river's behavior – like NASA engineers & iPhones).
● Tension: Human systems are fragile. Egypt: bondage vs. plenty (leeks, onions – at least
you didn’t die of thirst).
○ Safety & slavery, abundance & affliction.
Stage 1: Upsetting the Equilibrium – The Sound of Thirst
● Scene: Endless, grinding walking. Water runs out.
○ Sound of Thirst: Silence, shuffling, creaking, then a low rumble (murmuring).
● The Cry (Ex. 17:3): "Why did you bring us out... to kill us... with thirst?"
● Perspectives:
○ Moses: Rock & hard place. God seems silent (coffee break).
○ People: Kids thirsty. Egypt had water. Following a man with a stick.
● Crucial Insight: 400 years of Egyptian theology – a god for everything (Anuket,
Renenutet, Khnum).
○ This Hebrew God: Not OF something, but God OF EVERYTHING. (Magnificent
or vague?)
Stage 2: The Nick Saban Problem – Great Resume, Unfamiliar Playbook
● Analogy: Nick Saban arrives in Alabama (Jan 2007).
○ Bulletproof resume (LSU, Belichick). Players knew stats, didn't know Saban.
○ Transformation: 6-6 to 12-0, National Champs. Got transformation, not just a
good coach.
● Israel & God: Knew His resume (Egypt, Red Sea, Manna). Didn't know His
system/playbook.
○ Delays ≠ denials. Testing ≠ abandonment. His methods ≠ Egyptian protocol.
● The Gap: No Bible app. Only stories. Their God = stranger with impressive references.
○ More hymns to Ra than prayers to Yahweh.
● Real Problem: Not thirst, but TRUST.
○ Massah (Testing) & Meribah (Quarreling).

● Mutual Testing: Israel tests if God shows up. God tests if Israel grows up.
○ Egyptian gods = transactional. This God = works through crisis, delay,
apprenticeship.
Stage 3: Disclosing the Key – The Real Question & Breathtaking Promise
● The Real Question: "Is the LORD among us or not?" (Ex. 17:7)
○ Challenges God’s providence, care, covenant, character.
○ Eden's question: Is God good? With us? Trusted when life is senseless?
○ Faith = constant renewal. Spiritual amnesia.
● The Promise (Ex. 17:6): "Behold, I will stand before you THERE on the rock at Horeb."
○ Not solutions, but PRESENCE. "I'll be there. ME."
● Contrast: Contactless World.
○ Personal Anecdote: Service station – no talking, no scanning, just pay & go
(almost shoplifted from Randy Savage).
○ Contactless delivery, payment, relationships, even church. Solutions without
presence.
○ DoorDash/Amazon drivers don’t stay.
● God's Way: "I don't do contactless. When you're in crisis... I show up PERSONALLY."
○ Question: How does an invisible God stand on a rock? (Let it hang)
Stage 4: Experiencing the Gospel – Let Them Feel It
● The Command: "Take your staff... Strike the rock."
○ That staff: Judgment (Nile to blood), Deliverance (Red Sea).
● The Scene: Moses, elders, Horeb (Mountain of God). Murmuring crowd, desperate
faces.
○ Moses raises staff. Collective breath held.
● The Impact: Wood meets stone... WATER!
○ Not blood, death, judgment. LIFE.
○ Not a trickle, a RIVER. Rescue.
○ Staff that crushed Egypt now raises Israel. Elders saw: "God was there."
● Connection 1: Festival of Booths (John 7:37-39)
○ Jerusalem, priests, Pool of Siloam, Isaiah's promise. "Hoshana!"
○ Jesus Interrupts: "If anyone is thirsty, let him come to ME and drink... rivers of
living water..."
○ John: "By this he meant the Spirit..."
● Connection 2: The Cross (John 19:34)
○ Six months later. Jesus crucified. Soldier's spear.
○ "At once, BLOOD and WATER flowed out." (Blood = atonement, Water = life).
● The Pattern:
○ Moses strikes rock -> physical life.
○ Soldier pierces Jesus -> eternal life.
○ Believers filled with Spirit -> rivers of living water flow outward.
○ The rock ONCE STRUCK becomes the source that NEVER STOPS FLOWING.

● Application - For Those Thirsty:
○ Spiritually dry? Rock struck, water flowing. Thirst draws Him.
○ Asking "Is God here?" (loss, confusion). He meets you on your rock of doubt.
○ Failed the test? (grumbled, doubted). Water flows. Grace ≠ performance. Rock
struck because we DIDN'T deserve it.
○ Clinging to hope? (healing, breakthrough). God NOT finished. Same power.
○ Thirsty for meaning/purpose? (Matt. 11:28) "Come to Me... I will give you rest."
○ Invitation: Come and drink. Come and rest. Come and live.
Stage 5: Anticipating Consequences – From Transaction to Grace
● Contrast: Nile (seasonal, limited) / Egyptian gods (transactional) VS. Holy Spirit:
○ Reliable & faithful ("not leave you orphans").
○ Eternal (not seasonal).
○ Unlimited (not by geography, race, ritual).
○ Personal (WITHIN us).
○ Stunning Difference: Not transactional. GRACE. Rock struck ONCE, for all,
forever.
● Moses Missed It (Numbers 20):
○ Thirsty again. God: "SPEAK to the rock."
○ Moses strikes (anger, frustration). Water flows (God's grace).
○ Moses forfeits Promised Land – acted as if first striking wasn't enough.
● The Gospel Reality: The Rock (Christ) ALREADY STRUCK. Judgment fallen. Water
flowing. Spirit given.
○ Christ = Intercessor. Don't strike Him again.
○ Just SPEAK. Just ASK. (Matt. 7:7).
○ Promise: "I am with you always..." (Matt. 28:20) – through the Spirit.
○ "Is the Lord among us?" -> No longer a question. It's a REALITY.
Conclusion & Altar Call: Don't Strike the Rock - Just Speak
● Our habit: trying to earn what's given. Striking what's struck. Hoarding daily manna.
● Stop Performing: Don't need to clean up first.
○ Just speak: "God, I'm thirsty. I'm dry. I can't make it."
● Message to those earning favor: ("Pray longer, read more, give more...")
○ Stop striking the Rock. He's ALREADY STRUCK.
● Message to those on old manna: (Living on past experiences, now critical).
○ His mercies NEW every morning. Fresh. Available NOW.
● Message to those looking back to Egypt: (Addiction, toxic relationships seemed
"better").
○ LIE from hell. Nothing in Egypt but slavery/death. Promised Land available.
● The Answer: "Is the Lord among us?" YES. Rock struck. Blood/water flowed. Spirit
given.
○ Not just AMONG us, WITHIN us.
● Invitation for the Thirsty: (Hope, healing, breakthrough, peace, something real).

○ Speak: "Jesus, I'm thirsty. I need You. I can't make it without You."
● Final Call: Don't figure it out, earn it, perform.
○ Just SPEAK. Just COME. Just DRINK.
○ Altar open. Gather. Collect. Fellowship with Holy Ghost.
○ Grace can't be earned, only RECEIVED.
○ Keep gathering till victory, restoration, salvation, peace.
○ Manna (ministry of Holy Ghost) IS AVAILABLE.
○ Don't leave thirsty.
○ COME. GATHER. DRINK. BE FILLED.
○ Just speak to the Rock. He's listening. Waiting. Ready to pour out.
