## Text check of the draft
Verified against the Greek of Mark 8:22-10:52:
- *thelō* in 8:34, 10:36, 10:51: correct ("What do you want me to do for you?" is the same question to James and John and to Bartimaeus).
- *hypage* ("Go") in 10:21 and 10:52: correct.
- *hodos* seven times in 8:27-10:52 (8:27; 9:33, 34; 10:17, 32, 46, 52): correct. *para tēn hodon* (10:46) to *en tē hodō* (10:52): correct.
- *epitimaō* in 8:30, 32, 33; 9:25; 10:13, 48: correct, and the draft rightly says not to call it unique.
- Three predictions / misunderstandings / teachings (8:31-38; 9:31-37; 10:32-45): correct.

Two things to fix:
1. **Length math.** The draft budgets 5,750 words for 32 minutes at 180 wpm. Kyle's measured pace (Mark 9, Oct 4) is about 110 *manuscript* words per minute, because he adds about half again live: 4,850 written words ran 43 minutes. A 5,750-word manuscript would likely run about 50-52 minutes. For 32 minutes, write about 3,500 words; for 40 minutes, about 4,400. Scale each movement's word count down by about 40% and keep the start minutes.
2. **"Walked with Jesus for three years"** (movement 4). Mark never gives a length for the ministry; the three-year figure comes from John's Passover count. Say "the men who had walked with Jesus all this way" instead.
