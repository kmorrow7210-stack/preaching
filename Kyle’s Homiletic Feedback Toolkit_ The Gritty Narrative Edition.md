### Kyle’s Homiletic Feedback Toolkit: The Gritty Narrative Edition

#### 1\. The Manifesto of the Gritty Narrative

Preaching is not a lecture; it is a cinematic event. If the congregation leaves with a set of abstract theological propositions but no blood on their hands or dirt under their fingernails, the sermon has failed. This toolkit is designed to strip away the "religious kitsch"—the pre-chewed sentiment and sanitized jargon—and replace it with "cinematic concrete" storytelling.The Gospel does not need an escort or a defense attorney. It needs a witness. We have a  **Zero Tolerance Policy**  for the following:

* **Defensive Apologies:**  Statements like "I’m sorry if this is hard to hear" or "I know this text is difficult."  
* **Throat-Clearing:**  Meandering introductions that delay the encounter with the Word.  
* **Rhetorical Backpedaling:**  Using "maybe," "in a sense," or "I think" to soften the blow of a divine declaration.The goal is Narrative Velocity—the ratio of sensory verbs to abstract nouns. We are here to perform the Gospel, not just describe it.

##### Dead Metaphor Detection

Identify religious clichés and provide a "Gritty Alternative." Dissect the corpse of the cliché and replace it with living tissue.

* **Religious Cliché:**  "Walking in the light."  
* **Gritty Alternative:**  "Navigating a high-beam glare in a midnight fog."  
* **Religious Cliché:**  "The harvest is plenty."  
* **Gritty Alternative:**  "Fields of wheat rotting in the rain because the blades are dull."  
* **Religious Cliché:**  "God's unconditional love."  
* **Gritty Alternative:**  "A father standing on the porch until his eyes burn, watching the road for a son who cursed him."

#### 5\. Framework 4: The Psychological Kill-Switch (Transactional Analysis)

Expose the psychological power-play. Eliminate "Critical Parent" moralizing and "Adapted Child" pleading. Demand a move toward  **Adult**  (rational reality) and  **Free Child**  (authentic wonder).

##### Ego State & Game Detection

* **Dominant Ego State:**  Identify: Parent, Adult, or Child  
* **Psychological Game Identified:**  
* *Blemish:*  Is the preacher finding small flaws in the congregation to maintain superiority?  
* *NIGYSOB (Now I've Got You):*  Is the preacher trapping the listener in guilt to force a "payoff"?  
* **Drama Triangle Role:**  Rescuer, Persecutor, or Victim

##### The Escape Route: Adult-to-Adult Pivot

* **The Problem:**  Identify where the preacher "smothered" the congregation as a Rescuer.  
* **The Scripted Pivot:**  Write a 2-3 sentence "Adult" rewrite that invites autonomous faith instead of compliance.

#### 6\. The Grading Rigor & The Penalty Box

##### Narrative Scoring

* **1–6: Narrative Failure.**  Too abstract, too many clichés, or psychologically manipulative.  
* **7–8: Street Ready.**  Good use of concrete imagery; God remains the Subject.  
* **9–10: Cinematic Mastery.**  Exceptional; the sermon performs what it proclaims with raw power.

##### The Penalty Box

* **Religious Kitsch (-2 pts):**  Using pre-chewed sentiment or "churchy" filler (e.g., "sweet fellowship").  
* **Defensive Apologies (-2 pts):**  Apologizing for the text’s difficulty or the sermon’s length.  
* **Theological Jargon (-2 pts):**  Using abstract terms (Logos, Justification, Sanctification) without "cinematic concrete" grounding.  
* **Constative Surplus (-2 pts):**  Talking  *about*  God for the entire sermon without ever speaking  *for*  Him.
