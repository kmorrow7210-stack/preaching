## Overview
This skill generates sermon outlines or full drafts based on the "Kyle M Storytelling Method." It avoids standard three-point exposition in favor of a narrative arc that bridges ancient text with modern life.

## The Framework
Always follow this 4-step sequence when drafting content:

### 1. The Foundational Hook (Grounding)
* **Goal:** Bridge the listener's world and the text's world immediately.
* **Action:** Start with a "universal human problem" grounded in a specific context.
* **Techniques:**
    * *Modern Analogy:* Connect a known event (e.g., Costa Concordia) to the text.
    * *Personal Anecdote:* Use self-deprecating humor or vulnerability.
    * *Relatable Experience:* Validate a specific emotion (e.g., "the scariest sound is silence").
    * *Deconstructed Cliché:* Flip a common phrase to reveal a theological deficit.

### 2. Re-Narrating the Text (Human Drama)
* **Goal:** Transform static history into visceral drama.
* **Action:** Retell the scripture focusing on internal thoughts, emotional conflicts, and sensory details.
* **Rules:**
    * Fill narrative gaps with plausible internal monologues (e.g., Abraham's doubt during the silence).
    * Translate ancient politics/customs into modern equivalents (e.g., "unsealed letter" = "viral tweet").
    * Avoid "Bible voice." Make the characters sound like real, stressed people.

### 3. Weaving the Grand Narrative (Typology)
* **Goal:** Resolve the tension through Christ, not moralism.
* **Action:** Frame the OT event/character as a direct "type" or shadow of Jesus.
* **Examples:**
    * *Isaac:* The ram's thicket = Christ's crown of thorns.
    * *Ishmael:* Dying of thirst = Jesus saying "I thirst."
    * *Eden:* Adam's failure to "keep" the garden = Jesus tearing the veil to restore access.

### 4. Redemptive Resolution
* **Goal:** Land the plane.
* **Action:** Connect the Christological fulfillment back to the Step 1 human crisis. Show how the Gospel answers the specific problem raised in the hook.

## Tone Guidelines
* **Style:** Immersive, relatable, interconnected.
* **Avoid:** "Simple exegesis," dry history, or moralizing without redemption.
* **Focus:** The single, unfolding drama of Scripture.
