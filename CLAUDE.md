# CLAUDE.md

This file tells Claude (Claude Code, Cowork, and Claude project threads) how to work in this repository. Read it before doing anything here.

## What this repo is for

This is Kyle Morrow's preaching workspace. Kyle is the pastor of Straight Creek Church of God (Fort Payne, AL), a Pentecostal congregation. The repo has three jobs:

1. **Put together sermons:** research, study the text, outline, draft, and prepare for the pulpit.
2. **Study preaching:** homiletics, the theology of preaching, Kyle's Th.M. and D.Min. work on Pentecostal preaching.
3. **Grow as a preacher:** review delivered sermons, compare them to the manuscript, and give honest feedback.

## Read first

1. `about-me.md`: who Kyle is, how he works, his theological lens, and his rules for Claude. These rules apply to everything in this repo.
2. `voice/Kyle Preaching Voice Profile.md`: how Kyle actually preaches, built from his sermon transcripts. Read it before drafting any manuscript, outline, illustration, or altar call in his voice. Run its closing checklist before calling a draft done.
3. The sermon's own project brief, when one exists (see below).

## Standing rules for sermon work

- **The project brief is the source of truth.** Each sermon has a brief (a `PROJECT_BRIEF.md` in its folder, or a Notion prep page). Kyle edits it himself. Follow it over anything else, and do not overwrite it.
- **Titles and illustrations are options until Kyle confirms them.** Offer them as choices, never as settled.
- **Translations:** the opening public Scripture reading is **KJV**. Every later Scripture reference in the sermon body is **NIV**, unless Kyle says otherwise.
- **Verify every Scripture quotation against the actual text**, never from memory, and give exact references.
- **Opening order is fixed:** Scripture reading, title, prayer, "you may be seated," then the hook (story or question), then the body. Never put the hook before the reading.
- **Length:** a Sunday sermon runs about 40 minutes. Kyle adds roughly half again when he preaches, so aim for about 4,500 to 5,500 written words (about 110 manuscript words per minute).
- **Text first:** the passage sets the agenda. Do the study before the writing. If the text has not been studied yet, start there.
- **Sources:** never put words in a scholar's mouth. Flag any claim that is not solidly sourced.
- **Voice:** plain, spoken, pulpit language in Kyle's own framework (Divine Council, Second Temple Judaism, Pentecostal pneumatology), not generic evangelical phrasing. Follow the voice rules in `about-me.md`, including no em dashes.

## How the repo is organized

Right now most files sit at the top level: past sermon manuscripts (`.md` and `.pdf`), study notes on preaching, the feedback toolkit, and dissertation material. Leave those where they are unless Kyle asks to reorganize.

For new work, use these folders:

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `sermons/YYYY-MM-DD Short Title/` | One folder per sermon: the brief, study notes, outline, drafts, and the delivered transcript |
| `study/` | Homiletics reading notes, seminary work, and preaching theory |
| `voice/` | The voice profile and anything about Kyle's delivery |

Name files plainly, with the date first when the date matters (for example `2026-10-11 Outline.md`).

## Boundaries

- Do not move, rename, or delete Kyle's files without his approval. Add new files instead.
- Do not publish, send, or upload anything outside this repo without his approval.
- Do work on a branch and open a pull request for Kyle to review. Do not push straight to `main` unless he asks.
- At the end of a productive session on a sermon, note what changed in that sermon's folder so the next session can pick up.
